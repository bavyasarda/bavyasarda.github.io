Academic research & publications

NLP · Financial AI 🏆 Ranked 4th / 9 Teams Accepted

QRAFT: QLoRA Retrieval-Augmented Fine-Tuning for Causal Span Extraction in Financial Documents

Conducted under the supervision of Sonal Dabral, Sony Research India, for the FinCausal 2026 shared task. Developed a state-of-the-art causal span extraction system that combines parameter-efficient fine-tuning with retrieval-augmented generation.

  • Fine-tuned Qwen3-4B-Instruct using QLoRA on 2K annotated financial instances for causal span extraction.
  • Integrated TF-IDF retrieval to guide generation and improve identification of cause-effect spans.
  • Achieved 4.76/5 LLM-as-a-judge score, demonstrating near-human alignment in causal reasoning.
  • Ranked 4th out of 9 competing teams in the international FinCausal 2026 shared task.
