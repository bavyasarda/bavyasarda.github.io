Published research

📄 Read on ACL Anthology ↗
Accepted & Published LREC 2026 · FNP Workshop 🏆 4th of 9 teams

QRAFT: QLoRA Retrieval-Augmented Fine-Tuning for Causal Span Extraction in Financial Documents

Bavya Sarda, Pulkit Chatwal, Sonal Dabra

Proceedings of the 7th Financial Narrative Processing Workshop (FNP) @ LREC 2026, pages 175–180 · Palma de Mallorca, Spain · May 16, 2026 · Supervised by Sonal Dabra, Sony Research India

  • Framed the FinCausal 2026 English shared task as instruction-tuned causal question answering, extracting verbatim cause–effect spans from financial paragraphs.
  • Fine-tuned Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 with 4-bit QLoRA on 2,000 annotated financial instances, reformatting data into the Qwen ChatML instruction template.
  • Guided inference with TF-IDF cosine retrieval of the most causally relevant sentence, plus constrained greedy decoding to enforce verbatim extraction and reduce hallucination.
  • Scored 4.76 / 5 under the official LLM-as-a-judge framework, ranking 4th out of nine teams on the English leaderboard.
